Privacy Policy

Cherry Sudoku
Effective date: November 15, 2026

Data disclosure review: This policy reflects the app's current device-only behavior and must be rechecked whenever data collection, advertising, analytics, or SDK behavior changes.

Information we collect

Cherry Sudoku does not ask you to create an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with the developer. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Information stored on your device

The app stores puzzle progress, your current puzzle entries, game settings, and internal app-version preferences on your device so that the game can work and remember your progress. The app does not send this information to the developer. Android device backup may back up app data according to your device and account settings; that backup is managed by Android and your device provider.

You can reset puzzle progress in the app's Settings. You can remove locally stored app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Existing device backups are controlled separately through Android settings.

Children's privacy

Cherry Sudoku is designed as a family-friendly puzzle game. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Permissions and third parties

The app does not request access to personal data or require an internet connection. It uses Android audio APIs to play optional game sound effects. No personal information is shared with third parties by the app.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy if the app's data practices change. The latest version will be available at the policy URL listed on the Google Play Store.
